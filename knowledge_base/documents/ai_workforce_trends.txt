AI Workforce Trends

Artificial intelligence is changing how work is performed across many industries. 
Routine and repetitive activities are increasingly supported or automated by AI systems.

Software development is becoming more AI-assisted. Developers increasingly need skills
in AI-assisted coding, code review, system design, testing and AI solution architecture.

Data and analytics roles are also changing. AI can assist with data analysis, visualization,
report generation and insight generation. Professionals increasingly need AI-assisted
analytics and advanced machine learning skills.

Software testing is becoming more automated. AI can generate test cases, identify defects
and support intelligent test automation. QA professionals therefore need stronger skills
in AI testing and intelligent automation.

Customer support is also being transformed. AI can handle repetitive support requests,
while human workers increasingly focus on complex problem solving, communication and
customer relationships.

Human capabilities remain important. Strategic thinking, leadership, communication,
judgment, creativity and problem solving continue to be valuable when AI is introduced.

Organizations need reskilling strategies to help employees move from routine tasks toward
AI-assisted and higher-value activities.
