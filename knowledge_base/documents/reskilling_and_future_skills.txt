Reskilling and Future Skills

The future workforce will require a combination of technical and human capabilities.

Important technical capabilities include AI-assisted development, machine learning,
AI testing, intelligent automation, AI security analytics, AI-assisted analytics and
AI solution architecture.

Employees affected by automation should be supported through targeted reskilling.
Reskilling priorities should consider the level of AI impact on current activities,
the importance of future capabilities and the degree to which existing skills are changing.

AI does not eliminate the need for human judgment. Communication, strategic thinking,
leadership, creativity, collaboration, ethical judgment and complex problem solving
remain important human capabilities.

Organizations can use workforce intelligence to identify declining skills, emerging
skills and roles that require the greatest reskilling attention.
