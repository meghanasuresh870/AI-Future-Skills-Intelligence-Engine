\# AI Future Skills Intelligence Engine



An AI-powered workforce intelligence platform that analyzes how Artificial Intelligence and digital transformation can change jobs, activities, and workforce skills.



\## 🎯 Objective



The system helps organizations understand:



\- What future skills they will need

\- Which current skills are declining due to automation

\- Which roles require the most reskilling

\- Why a particular skill or role is affected by AI



\## 🔗 Intelligence Flow



The core intelligence connects:



\*\*Process → Activity → Role → Current Skill → AI Impact → Future Skill\*\*



This allows the system to move beyond simple skill lists and understand how AI changes actual work activities.



\## ✨ Key Features



\- Executive workforce intelligence dashboard

\- Future skill identification

\- Declining skill analysis

\- Role reskilling prioritization

\- Role-level skill gap analysis

\- AI impact classification

\- Explainable AI recommendations

\- RAG-based knowledge retrieval

\- Gemini-powered natural language analysis

\- Interactive Streamlit interface



\## 🧠 AI Impact Categories



The system analyzes workforce skills using categories such as:



\- Emerging

\- Increasing

\- AI-Augmented

\- Changing

\- Declining

\- Enduring Human Capability



\## 🤖 AI \& RAG



The application combines structured workforce intelligence with a knowledge base.



The structured dataset provides the quantitative intelligence, while the RAG pipeline retrieves relevant workforce information to support AI-generated explanations.



Gemini is used for natural-language analysis and executive-level responses.



\## 📊 Application Modules



\### Executive Overview

Provides an overall view of AI impact across workforce skills and activities.



\### Future Skills

Identifies skills that are becoming important because of AI adoption.



\### Declining Skills

Highlights current skills affected by automation.



\### Reskilling

Ranks roles according to their reskilling requirements.



\### Role Intelligence

Provides role-specific activity, current skill, AI impact, and future skill mappings.



\### Ask AI

Allows users to ask workforce intelligence questions in natural language.



Example questions:



\- What are the top future skills our organisation will need?

\- Which skills are declining because of automation?

\- Which roles require the greatest reskilling?

\- Why does the system believe this?
